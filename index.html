Juni and the Sleeping Giants

Someone has stolen the Lullaby Bell, and the giants under the hills are stirring. Help Juni, an apprentice bell-ringer, and Clank, her grumpy talking clapper, follow the thief across the town of Kettleby, solving puzzles and finding the Lost Notes along the way.

Coming soon to iPhone, iPad and Android. Acts 1 and 2 are free, with no ads.
