Common questions

I’m stuck. How do I get a hint?

Tap Clank’s face in the bar at the top of the screen. He’ll give you a nudge towards what to do next. If you’re still stuck, ask him again for a clearer one. Juni’s notebook (the book button) also lists your current goals and the clues you’ve found.

How is my progress saved?

The game saves automatically after every scene and every time you change place. Choose Continue on the title screen to carry on from where you left off. You can also save to one of four slots yourself from the menu (the button at the top right).

How do I play Act 2?

Act 2 is free. At the end of Act 1, tap Unlock the next acts, then Unlock next to Act 2. You can also do this from Unlock acts on the title screen. Then choose Continue to Act 2.

When are Acts 3 to 5 coming?

They’re being made now. They aren’t on sale yet, and the game shows them as coming soon.

The text is too small, or the sound is too loud.

Open the menu (top right), then Settings. Text Size makes all the writing larger, and there are separate volume sliders for music, sound effects and voices. Auto dialogue moves the conversation on by itself.

Does the game need the internet? Does it have ads?

No to both. The whole game plays offline. There are no ads, no accounts, and the game collects no data. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone or tablet. Can I move my saves?

Saves are kept only on the device you play on, and we can’t see or recover them. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets. The game plays in landscape.

Reporting a problem

If something goes wrong, please email amaltasgames@concinnity.in and tell us:

  • your device (for example, iPhone 15 or Samsung Galaxy Tab S9) and its system version,
  • where you were in the game and what you were trying to do,
  • what happened, and a screenshot if you can.
