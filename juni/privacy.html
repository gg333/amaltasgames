Privacy Policy

Juni and the Sleeping Giants

Effective 24 October 2026

In short: the game collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Juni and the Sleeping Giants (“the game”) is made by Amaltas Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game on Android.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information. That includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, contacts, photos, voice recordings, device identifiers and advertising IDs.

The game asks for no Android permissions and does not use the internet.

Saved games

Your progress and settings (such as volume) are saved in the game’s own storage on your device. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The game shows no advertising and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software. We do not sell or share any information, because we don’t have any.

Google Play

You download the game from Google Play, which is run by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. Google Play gives developers summary statistics, such as how many times the game has been installed, and anonymous crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers. These do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the game.

Children

The game is made for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Purchases

The game has no purchases at present. If a later version offers purchases, they will be handled by Google Play, and we will update this policy before that version is released.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
